General information

Eligibility

People living in Canada may view and change their address, telephone number and payment destination online.

Exceptions:

  • If you have an address outside Canada, you can only view your information online. It is not yet possible to change foreign addresses or direct deposit online. Please contact us to update your information.
  • If you receive children's benefits and are between the ages of 18 and 25, you may not be able to access this online service due to system limitations. If you wish to make changes, please contact us.
  • If you receive a Quebec Pension Plan benefit, please contact Retraite Québec for more information.

Making changes

Updating other information, such as name, marital status or date of birth

These services may be available online in the future, but are not at this time. Please contact us to make these types of changes.

Approving updates

After you check that the information you entered is correct, select "Confirm" to complete your update. Online, your changes will show immediately. Please review them.

Selecting "Cancel" will end your session. You will remain logged in, but your changes will not be saved or recorded.

Postal code format

Please enter the postal code of your new address. Postal codes are six characters long and are always in letter-number format, such as: A1A 1A1, (where A = any letter and 1 = any number). If you have trouble, check that the letters "O" or lowercase "L" are not substituted for zeros or ones, or vice-versa. If you do not know your postal code you can look it up on the Canada Post web site. At this time, postal codes can only be updated online for Canadian addresses. Please contact us for changes to addresses outside Canada.

Mandatory fields

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) must be completed. Some fields may be filled in based on the postal code you entered. To change the information, select "Enter a different postal code" or "Enter a different address for this postal code".

Number suffix

Some addresses include a suffix, for example: 123 ½ Main St., where ½ is the suffix and not an apartment number. If you do not have a number suffix, please leave this field blank.

Delivery mode

If you have mail delivery to your door, please select "None" from the pull-down menu.

Otherwise, select from the following methods of mail delivery:

  • MR - mobile route is a variation on door-to-door delivery, when street is served by a delivery route service.
  • PO Box - post office box
  • RR - rural route is a delivery route service from a delivery installation.
  • GD - general delivery is mail held at a post office until it is picked up in person.
  • SS - suburban service is mail delivery to group mail boxes; usually found near, or on the edge of, urban areas.

Address length

The database where address information is stored allows for a maximum of 30 characters per line, including spaces and punctuation. If longer, your address must be shortened. Note: Street types such as Blvd (Boulevard) and Ave (Avenue) are part of your address and should not be removed.

Entering a future effective date

When you select "Change" to enter your new information, you can either accept today's date as shown, or enter an effective date up to 180 days in the future. When choosing a future effective date, you must ensure the date you are choosing is one month greater than the month you are currently in.

For example, if you want your January payment to be sent to a new destination, you must have an effective date of no later than December 31.

If you provide the information close to, or past the first day of the month, you might have missed the cut-off date. Contact us to check that your change will take effect on the desired date before making any changes.

Branch and institution numbers

  • The branch number is a code that identifies the location of your financial institution. It appears at the bottom of your cheques:
    • On personalized cheques, this code is the second series of numbers from the left.
    • On non-personalized cheques, this code is the first series of numbers from the left.
  • The institution number is a code that identifies the name of your financial institution. This code appears at the bottom of your cheques:
    • On personalized cheques, this code is the third series of numbers from the left.
    • On non-personalized cheques, this code is the second series of numbers from the left.
  • The institution and branch numbers can also be found inside your bank book, on your bank statements or by contacting your financial institution.
  • Do not use the numbers on your bank or debit cards.

Entering direct deposit information

Cheque number: If you use personalized cheques, the first set of numbers is your three-digit personal cheque number. Do not enter this number.

Branch number: The second set of numbers on personalized cheques, or the first set of numbers on non-personalized cheques, is your five-digit branch number. Enter this number in the first entry field.

Institution number: The next set of numbers is your three-digit institution number. Enter this number in the second entry field.

Account number: The last set of numbers is your bank account number. The number of digits varies depending on the financial institution. Enter your account number in the third entry field.

Telephone number

International telephone number format

If you enter an international telephone number, you must include a country code.

Enter the country code up to five digits, e.g. 12345.

If your telephone number is in Canada or the United States, do not enter a country code.

Telephone number format

When you select to add a telephone number you have the option to include a home, mobile and work number.

Please enter your three-digit area code, followed by a seven-digit phone number (e.g. 123-4567890).

The area code must have three digits. If your area code is less than 3 numbers, enter "0" in front of the area code.

Work telephone number extension format

When you select to add a new work number, you will have the option to include an extension.

Enter your extension number up to five digits, e.g. 12345.

Payment information

Gross entitlement amount

The amount shown represents the gross entitlement in Canadian funds. This is the amount owed to you before any deductions such as non-resident tax or voluntary tax withholds.

If applicable, the child rearing provision is factored into the calculation of this amount.

Since January 2019, the CPP has been enhanced for those who have worked and made contributions to the CPP in 2019 or later. The enhancement increases CPP retirement, disability and survivor’s pensions as well as the post-retirement benefit. If the enhancement applies to you, it will be factored into the calculation of your gross CPP benefit amount.

Types of deductions

Refers to one of the following deduction types:

  • Administrative penalty - a fine charged to someone who knowingly gives Service Canada false or misleading information, or leaves out information related to their benefit.
  • Canada Revenue Agency recovery tax - to recover an income tax debt.
  • Employment Insurance recovery - to recover an Employment Insurance debt.
  • Family Orders Enforcement Payment - related to the Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act.
  • Family orders enforcement fees - to pay family court proceedings related to the Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act.
  • Non-resident tax - taxes on benefits paid to people who live outside Canada. The amount varies from 0 to 25 percent of the payment amount, depending on the country in which you live.
  • OAS pension recovery tax - repayment of some or all of your OAS pension, if your net income is more than a certain amount for the year.
  • Overpayment recovery - repayment of an overpayment.
  • Voluntary federal tax - a percentage or amount you asked to be deducted each month for income tax purposes.
  • Voluntary provincial tax - an amount you asked be deducted each month for income tax purposes.
  • Veterans Affairs Canada recovery - to recover a Veterans Affairs Canada debt.

Types of reimbursements

Refers to one of the following:

  • Reimbursement to private insurer - for months you were eligible for CPP disability benefit and private insurance entitlement.
  • Reimbursement to province or municipality - for assistance you received from a provincial or municipal authority while you were waiting for a decision about your benefit application.
  • Reimbursement to Veterans Affairs Canada - for assistance you received from Veterans Affairs Canada while you were waiting for a decision about your benefit application.

Net payment amount

The payment Service Canada is issuing you, in Canadian dollars, after any applicable deductions.

Payment status

Refers to one of the following:

  • Issued - your payment has been processed and soon will be mailed to you or deposited into your account.
  • Paid - your payment was mailed to you or deposited into your account.

Payment method

Refers to one of the following:

  • Cheque
  • Direct deposit

Payment frequency

By default, your payment will be directly deposited to your bank account every month. If you didn’t choose direct deposit, a cheque will be mailed to you during the last 3 business days of each month. View payment dates.

If you have requested that your payment be adjusted to bi-monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually, your payment dates will be adjusted accordingly.

Retrieving your banking information from CRA

For Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) and the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) to share your personal and direct deposit information, your consent is required.

By selecting "I agree", you confirm that you have read and understood the ESDC Privacy Notice Statement and that you agree with these two statements:

  • I consent to the CRA sharing with ESDC my direct deposit information used for income tax refunds, working income tax benefit advance payments and GST/HST credit payments. The information will be used to make Canada Pension Plan payments to me, until I tell ESDC to stop using it for my CPP payments.
  • I consent to ESDC sharing with the CRA my Social Insurance Number, last name, and date of birth so that the CRA can identify me correctly.

If you select "I do not agree", your information will not be shared. You may still update your direct deposit information for your CPP payments using your My Service Canada Account.

For more details about the handling of your personal information by ESDC, please see the ESDC's Privacy Notice Statement.

For more details about the handling of your personal information by the CRA, please see the CRA's Banking Information Privacy Notice.
