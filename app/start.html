Information

Please be aware that Service Canada receives all online applications from My Service Canada Account in Moncton, New Brunswick. As a result, your online application will be received and recorded by the Service Canada Moncton office in the Atlantic Standard Time.

If applying on the last day of the month, the time of day you submit your online application could affect the start date of your monthly benefits.
